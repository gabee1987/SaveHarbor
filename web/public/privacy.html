Privacy Policy

SaveHarbor and SaveHarbor Cloud · Last updated: 22 October 2026

SaveHarbor is a free, non-commercial Windows application that backs up game saves and lets a small group of friends share game worlds through SaveHarbor Cloud. This policy explains which personal data SaveHarbor processes, why, where, and for how long, and which rights you have under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Controller

[OPERATOR NAME], a private individual operating SaveHarbor as a hobby project.
Contact: [CONTACT E-MAIL]

2. Data processed

Data Purpose
E-mail address Identifies your account and receives your sign-in codes. It is never shown to other members and never used for newsletters or advertising.
Name and profile picture address from Google (only if you choose Continue with Google) Sent by Google with every Google sign-in and stored with your account by the sign-in service. SaveHarbor does not display or otherwise use them.
Player name you enter, per group Shown to the other members of that group.
Device name (by default the name of your PC; changeable in Settings › Sharing) and a random device identifier Shows the group which PC is playing a world or uploaded a version, so that two players do not host the same world at once.
Groups, memberships and invite codes Lets you join and leave groups and decides who may see a group's worlds.
Uploaded game worlds (save files), their versions, sizes, times and uploader The sharing service itself: other members of the group can download them.
Play sessions (who is playing which world, since when) Prevents conflicting play and shows the group who is playing.
Online status while SaveHarbor is open (can be turned off with Show me as online) Shows your group who has SaveHarbor open. It is not stored after you close the app.
IP address and technical request data Processed by the hosting providers when your device connects, to deliver the service and protect it against abuse.

SaveHarbor has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The application's log file stays on your PC; it does not contain sign-in codes, tokens or other members' data.

3. Google user data

If you choose Continue with Google, SaveHarbor asks Google only for the basic sign-in scopes openid, email and profile. SaveHarbor uses your Google e-mail address solely to identify your SaveHarbor account, so that the same person is recognised on every device and with e-mail code sign-in. The name and profile picture address that Google includes are stored with your account by the sign-in service and are not used, shown or shared. SaveHarbor does not access your Google Drive, Gmail, contacts or any other Google data, does not keep Google's access tokens, does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising, and does not transfer it to anyone except the service providers listed below that run the service. You can remove SaveHarbor's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; deleting your account (section 8) also deletes the Google data stored with it.

SaveHarbor's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Legal basis

Processing is necessary to provide the service you request by creating an account or joining a group (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Protecting the service against abuse is based on legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

5. Service providers and location

  • Supabase (database, sign-in, file storage, live updates): hosts all SaveHarbor Cloud data in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany) and sends the sign-in e-mails.
  • Google: only if you choose Continue with Google. Google then learns that you sign in to SaveHarbor.
  • GitHub: hosts this companion page and the application's installer and updates.

These providers process data under their own data processing terms. Where a provider transfers data outside the European Economic Area, it relies on the safeguards of Chapter V GDPR, such as standard contractual clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

6. Storage on your device

The SaveHarbor application keeps your sign-in on your PC, encrypted for your Windows user account. This companion page keeps your sign-in in your browser's local storage, and for up to 10 minutes the address you are signing in with, so that a reload does not interrupt the sign-in. No cookies are used for tracking.

7. Retention

  • Your account and e-mail address: until you ask for your account to be deleted.
  • Membership and player name: until you leave the group or the group is deleted.
  • Uploaded worlds and their versions: until a member removes the world from the group's cloud, or the group is deleted.
  • Play sessions: until the session ends or expires, usually within minutes after play.
  • Sign-in codes: valid for 10 minutes and usable once.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive it in a portable format (Art. 15–21 GDPR). To exercise these rights, including deleting your account, write to the contact address above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, in particular in the EU country where you live; in Hungary this is the Nemzeti Adatvédelmi és Információszabadság Hatóság (NAIH, naih.hu).

9. Children

SaveHarbor is not directed at children under 16. Do not create an account if you are younger.

10. Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new date.
