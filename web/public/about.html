SaveHarbor

Game save backup and world sharing for friends

SaveHarbor is a free Windows application for players who share a game world with friends. It backs up your game saves automatically, and with SaveHarbor Cloud it lets a group of friends take turns hosting the same world: the newest version is downloaded before you play and shared again when you close the game, so nobody plays on an old copy and two players never host the same world at once.

What SaveHarbor does

  • Backups: keeps restorable copies of your worlds on your PC, also before SaveHarbor replaces a world with a newer version.
  • Sharing in a group: create a group, invite friends with an invite code, and share worlds with them.
  • One-click Play: checks for a newer version, starts the game, and uploads your progress when the game closes.
  • Who is playing: shows which friend is hosting a world right now, in the app and on the companion page.
  • Companion page: a read-only page for your phone that shows your group's worlds and who is playing.

Signing in

SaveHarbor Cloud needs an account so that only members of a group can see its worlds. You can sign in with a one-time code sent to your e-mail address, or with Continue with Google. With Google, SaveHarbor receives only your e-mail address to identify your account, together with the name and profile picture address that Google includes; it never sees your Google password and has no access to your Google Drive, Gmail or other Google data. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Supported games

Windrose, RuneScape: Dragonwilds and Enshrouded on Windows (Steam). SaveHarbor is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the publishers of these games, by Valve or by Google.
